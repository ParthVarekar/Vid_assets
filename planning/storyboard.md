# Storyboard — "Off the Page"

30 fps · 100 BPM · 1 beat = 18 frames · 1 bar = 72 frames · 25 bars = 1,800 frames = 60.0s

Positions below describe the 16:9 master. The 9:16 and 1:1 versions use the same scenes with the text block stacked above the stage and lines broken more often. In every format the line enters at the middle of the left edge and leaves at the middle of the right edge, which is what lets the camera travel along it without a cut.

Persistent in every scene: the notebook header (quill mark, "ProtoVinci", folio number, a thin rule) and a paper-grain overlay.

---

## Scene 1 — Hook · bars 1–3 · frames 0–216 · 0.0–7.2s

**Picture.** Warm paper. Faint construction geometry (a large circle arc and two rules in taupe) is already on the page. The quill comes down and touches the paper left of centre.

**Beats**

| Beat | Frame | Event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.1 | 0 | Paper, header, geometry. Quill descends. |
| 1.2 | 18 | Nib touches. The line starts from the left edge. "Some ideas" rises through a mask. |
| 1.4 | 54 | "shouldn't stay ideas." rises. |
| 2.1 | 72 | The line sweeps under the sentence as an underline, the quill riding its head. |
| 2.1–3.2 | 72–162 | Hold. Sentence complete and still for 3 seconds; slow drift. |
| 3.3 | 180 | The line runs on to the right edge; camera begins to follow. |

**Text.** "Some ideas shouldn't stay ideas." — Switzer Semibold, ink, two lines.

**Sound.** Paper tone. Chalk scratch tied to the line's speed. One soft note as each line of text lands.

**Out.** Lateral push right, following the line.

---

## Scene 2 — Sketch, then Build · bars 4–10 · frames 216–720 · 7.2–24.0s

### 2a Sketch · bars 4–5 · 216–360

**Picture.** Still paper. On the stage (right two-thirds), the line sketches a rough wireframe: an unsteady rectangle, a bar across its top, a crossed box for an image, squiggles for text, a small lozenge for a button. Construction lines extend past the corners as in a working drawing.

**Text.** Left: a mono note, "fig. 01 — the thing nobody's built yet".

**Sound.** Chalk strokes, a rising four-note figure, a filtered swell. Half a beat of silence before bar 6.

### The snap · bar 6 beat 1 · frame 360

On one downbeat: the line straightens and becomes exact; the page brightens from cream to near-white; the wireframe's squiggles and boxes become real components; the mono note is replaced by the word **Build.** Sound: tight impact, the pulse begins.

### 2b Build · bars 6–10 · 360–720

**Picture.** The rectangle is now a browser window on a tilted plane with a soft shadow. Inside, a site assembles on the beat: navigation, headline, supporting line, button, image panel. At bar 8 a phone slides out from behind the window showing the same product on mobile. At bar 9 a dashboard panel rises on the other side: a small table and a chart whose curve is drawn by the line.

**Figure labels (mono, with leader lines).** "fig. 02 — web app", "fig. 03 — mobile", "fig. 04 — internal tools".

**Text.** **Build.** with "websites · web apps · mobile · custom software" beneath in mono.

**Beats**

| Beat | Frame | Event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6.1 | 360 | Snap. "Build." lands. |
| 6.3–7.4 | 396–486 | Site components lock in, one per beat. |
| 8.1 | 504 | Phone slides out. fig. 03. |
| 9.1 | 576 | Dashboard rises. fig. 04. Line draws the chart curve. |
| 10.1–10.4 | 648–720 | Hold on the three artefacts; line exits right. |

**Sound.** Pad, round kick, plucked figure. A tuned tick as each component locks.

**Out.** Push right; the page darkens as the next scene arrives.

## Scene 3 — Automate · bars 11–15 · frames 720–1080 · 24.0–36.0s

**Picture.** Ink-black ground with a faint dot grid. The line, now bright sanguine, enters and becomes the main path of a workflow. Nodes appear on it in order: **Form → AI agent → Document → Email → CRM**, with one branch to **Sheet**. Small marks of work travel along the line and each node acknowledges them as they pass.

At bar 13 the AI agent node opens into a small panel: three lines of reasoning resolve, then three outputs fan out. At bar 14 a short list of manual tasks ticks itself off: data entry, reports, certificates, follow-ups.

**Text.** **Automate.** in white, with "AI agents · workflows · integrations" in mono.

**Beats**

| Beat | Frame | Event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 11.1 | 720 | Dark arrives. "Automate." lands. Line enters. |
| 11.3–12.4 | 756–864 | Nodes appear along the line, one per beat. |
| 13.1 | 864 | Agent panel opens; reasoning lines resolve. |
| 14.1 | 936 | Task list ticks off, one per half-beat. |
| 15.1–15.4 | 1008–1080 | Hold; work keeps flowing; line exits right. |

**Sound.** Drier, sequenced sixteenths. A ping in key each time work reaches a node.

**Out.** Push right.

## Scene 4 — Scale · bars 16–19 · frames 1080–1368 · 36.0–45.6s

**Picture.** Still dark. One unit (a small tile holding a miniature of the workflow) sits on the stage. On each bar it multiplies: 1, then 4, then 16, then a field that runs past the frame, as the camera pulls back. The line threads up through the field as a rising curve and climbs out toward the top right.

**Text.** **Scale.** with "cloud · systems that grow with you" in mono. No numbers.

**Beats**

| Beat | Frame | Event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 16.1 | 1080 | "Scale." lands. One unit. |
| 17.1 | 1152 | Four. |
| 18.1 | 1224 | Sixteen. The curve begins to climb. |
| 19.1 | 1296 | The field fills the frame; the curve reaches the edge. |
| 19.3 | 1332 | Rise into the change of scene. |

**Sound.** Layers stack each bar; the figure doubles an octave up; a swell into bar 20.

**Out.** Push right; the ground returns from ink to paper.

---

## Scene 5 — Proof · bars 20–21 · frames 1368–1512 · 45.6–50.4s

**Picture.** Paper again. A row of real shipped project screens on a tilted plane drifts slowly across, each with a mono caption giving its type and stated delivery time from the brand's site (for example "Product catalogue · 2 weeks"). The line runs beneath the row like a ground line.

**Text.** "One team. Idea to launch." with "Shipped in weeks." beneath.

**Sound.** Drums out. Pad and melody only: the breath before the ending.

**Out.** Push right.

---

## Scene 6 — Reveal and call to action · bars 22–25 · frames 1512–1800 · 50.4–60.0s

**Picture.** Clean paper, centred composition.

| Beat | Frame | Event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 22.1 | 1512 | The line arrives at centre and curls into the cradle stroke of the logo. On the downbeat the quill mark resolves in ink above it. |
| 22.3 | 1548 | "ProtoVinci" sets beneath the mark. |
| 23.1 | 1584 | "Creativity, Engineered." rises. The line settles as a short underline. Hold for a full bar. |
| 24.1 | 1656 | The lockup moves up and reduces; "Got a vision? Let's ship it." rises. |
| 24.3 | 1692 | The URL appears in a pill; "Build. Automate. Scale." in mono beneath. |
| 25.1–25.4 | 1728–1800 | Hold. The final frame is the poster. |

**Sound.** A gap, then the fullest chord of the film on 22.1. Long tail. One last soft tick as the URL appears.
