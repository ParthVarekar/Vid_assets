# Creative strategy — "Off the Page"

A 60-second brand film for ProtoVinci, with social versions and a 15-second cutdown.

## The idea in one line

**Some ideas shouldn't stay ideas.** A single red-chalk line, drawn by ProtoVinci's quill, leaves the notebook page and becomes working software: built, automated, scaled.

## Why this idea

It comes out of what the brand already is, so nothing has to be invented or borrowed.

- **The name.** Proto-type plus da Vinci, the artist-engineer whose notebooks hold machines that stayed on paper. ProtoVinci's promise is the opposite: the sketch gets built.
- **The logo.** A quill with a pen nib. In the film it is the instrument that draws the line, so the brand's most distinctive asset is on screen and working from the first second.
- **The tagline.** "Creativity, Engineered" becomes a visible event: the hand-drawn line snaps into precise geometry.
- **The brand's best copy.** The opening line is the brand's own pinned post, word for word.

## Positioning and message

- **Positioning:** the one team that takes an idea from sketch to shipped system.
- **Audience:** owner-led businesses, institutions and founders who can describe what they need but have no team to build it. Decision-makers, on a phone, usually muted.
- **What they should think afterwards:** "They could build the thing I have in my head, and they'd ship it quickly."
- **Tone:** confident, plain, unhurried. No hype words, no jargon, no claims we cannot stand behind.

## The device: one line, two states

| State | Looks like | Means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sketch | Red chalk (sanguine), slightly unsteady, on warm paper, with notebook marginalia | The idea, still only an idea |
| Engineered | Clean, exact, constant width, corners true | The same idea, now built |

The line is continuous for the whole film. The camera travels along it from left to right, so the film reads as one long notebook spread with no hard cuts. In every scene the line is the subject:

1. It underlines the opening statement.
2. It sketches a rough wireframe, "the thing nobody's built yet".
3. It snaps true and becomes the outline of a real interface (**Build**).
4. It becomes the path of a workflow, with work travelling along it (**Automate**).
5. It becomes a rising curve across a multiplying system (**Scale**).
6. It runs under real shipped work.
7. It draws home into the quill, and the brand name sets.

Capabilities are never listed. They appear as things being made, labelled the way a notebook labels figures: "fig. 02 — web app".

## Structure and arc

| Act | Bars | Time | Feeling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 1–3 | 0.0–7.2s | Quiet. Paper, one line, one sentence. |
| Sketch | 4–5 | 7.2–12.0s | Curiosity. The idea takes rough shape. |
| Build | 6–10 | 12.0–24.0s | The snap. Confidence, things locking into place. |
| Automate | 11–15 | 24.0–36.0s | The dark engine room. Work doing itself. |
| Scale | 16–19 | 36.0–45.6s | Momentum, widening. |
| Proof | 20–21 | 45.6–50.4s | A breath. Real work, human scale. |
| Reveal and CTA | 22–25 | 50.4–60.0s | Arrival. Name, promise, invitation. |

The tempo is 100 BPM, which makes one beat exactly 18 frames and one bar exactly 72 frames at 30fps. The film is 25 bars, 1,800 frames, 60.0 seconds. Every picture event is placed in beats.

## Visual language

**Colour.** Taken from the brand's own LinkedIn cover and website, plus one justified accent.

| Role | Colour | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper | Warm cream | `#EEEBE4` |
| Paper shade / construction lines | Taupe | `#A69A8A`, `#DFD8CE` |
| Ink | Near-black | `#09090B` |
| Interface surfaces | White, zinc greys | `#FFFFFF`, `#E4E4E7`, `#9898A3` |
| The line | Sanguine (red chalk) | `#C4472F`; on dark `#E4573B` |

Sanguine is the only colour that is not already in the brand. It is the chalk Leonardo drew in, it reads as "by hand", and it appears on exactly one thing: the idea. Everything built is in the brand's monochrome. The final frames are pure brand: black mark and wordmark on paper.

**Type.** Switzer Semibold with the site's tight tracking for statements; Switzer Medium for supporting lines; Geist Mono for figure labels and the notebook header. One family, two weights, plus mono.

**Composition.** Generous margins. A persistent notebook header carries the quill mark, the name and a folio number, so the brand is present in every frame. Interfaces sit on a tilted plane with soft shadows, echoing the tilted project cards on the brand's own website. Thin construction circles and rules in taupe echo the LinkedIn cover.

**Light and dark.** The film starts and ends in warm paper. Only Automate goes dark, as the "engine room"; the contrast gives the middle of the film its own identity and makes the return to paper feel like arrival.

**Motion.** Fast in, settle, hold. Exponential ease-out; no bounce on type. Words rise through a mask. Nothing is ever fully still: every hold carries a slow drift. Scene changes are lateral camera moves along the line with a short directional blur. The single exception to the calm is the snap at bar 6, which changes line, colour, type and sound together on one downbeat.

## Sound identity

- **Hook:** near silence. Paper room tone, chalk on paper, one soft felt-like note per phrase.
- **The snap:** a half-beat of silence, then a tight impact and the pulse arrives.
- **Build:** warm pad, round kick, plucked figure in D minor moving to B-flat and F.
- **Automate:** drier and more sequenced; ticking sixteenths; pings in key as work travels the line.
- **Scale:** layers stack, figure doubles at the octave, rise into bar 20.
- **Proof:** drums drop out; pad and melody only.
- **Reveal:** a gap, then the fullest chord of the film on the downbeat as the mark lands; long tail under the call to action.

A small reusable vocabulary (draw, tick, lock, travel, whoosh, land), all tuned to the score's key. No narration.

## Deliverables and why these formats

| Deliverable | Format | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Master | 16:9, 1920×1080, 60s | Website hero, YouTube, LinkedIn, presentations |
| Vertical | 9:16, 1080×1920, 60s, laid out natively | Reels, Shorts, Stories |
| Square | 1:1, 1080×1080, 60s, laid out natively | LinkedIn and X feed |
| Cutdown | 16:9 and 9:16, 15s | Paid placements, profile pins, muted autoplay |

Research on B2B video shows shorter cuts earn slightly better recognition and that most viewing is muted and mobile, which is why the vertical and the 15-second versions are full deliverables rather than afterthoughts.

## What this film will not do

No stock footage, no services list, no icon grids or technology logos, no Renaissance pastiche, no invented metrics, no testimonial quotes, no narrator.
